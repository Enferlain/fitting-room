# Candidate scores

Use the scores as a comparison aid. Ratings are judgment calls for these designs and their current authoring costs. 1 is low and 5 is high in each column. For setup cost, 5 means more expensive. Public-event risk describes how much the source discloses. Variant risk accounts for the specific contract and whether ordinary source recovery would solve it. A low contamination-resistance score for a pristine-restorable repair is possible even when its motivating event was obscure.
